# References

Bibliographic verification date: 23 October 2026. All 14 entries refer to published, peer-reviewed journal or conference versions. The identifiers R01–R14 are used in the [critical review and analysis table](README.md).

The DOI records were checked through the Crossref REST API for title, author list, venue, publication dates, volume and locator. Publisher records or author manuscripts were used to check the research content. Bibliographic metadata verifies identity and publication details; peer-review status is established by the publication venue, not by DOI registration alone.

The citation year follows the journal issue year where an issue date is available. R14 appeared online in 2017 and in the 2018 journal issue. R13 was published in 2023 even though its DOI contains 2022. R03 and R14 fall outside the five-year window and are retained as background on FHIR and synthetic records.
